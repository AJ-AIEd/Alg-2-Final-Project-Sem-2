How should students use this?

This tool supports the investigation journal. It does not replace it.

  • Track revisions and changed assumptions.
  • Compare assumptions, rates, constraints, and model versions.
  • Document model changes and what caused them.
  • Annotate reasoning as it evolves.
  • Record limitations, hidden variables, and uncertainty.
  • Connect graph ↔ algebra ↔ context.
  • Keep visible evidence of mathematical thinking.
